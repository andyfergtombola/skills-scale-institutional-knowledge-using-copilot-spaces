# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme's project management process documentation. This collection of guides centralizes our approach to delivering cross-functional projects with clarity, quality, and customer focus. Whether you're new to OctoAcme or looking to refresh your understanding of our workflows, these documents provide the foundation for how we run projects from concept to completion.

OctoAcme's project management lifecycle follows five core phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Retrospective**. During Initiation, teams validate business needs, identify stakeholders, and create a lightweight project one-pager with success metrics and timelines. Planning transforms approved initiatives into actionable backlogs with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, and release milestones. Execution focuses on day-to-day delivery through iterative sprints, daily standups, weekly syncs, and continuous quality validation. Release ensures features reach production safely with proper testing, deployment checklists, and rollback plans. Finally, Retrospectives capture learnings and drive continuous improvement through actionable feedback loops.

Our process is built around core personas with clearly defined responsibilities: **Project Managers** coordinate delivery, manage risks, and facilitate communication; **Product Managers** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success; **Developers** implement features, maintain tests, and collaborate on design; **QA Leads** define quality standards and coordinate testing activities; **Risk Managers** oversee risk identification and mitigation; **Communication Coordinators** manage meetings and stakeholder communications; and **Onboarding Specialists** guide new team members through processes and documentation. Supporting roles include Stakeholders who provide inputs and approvals. Clear ownership and responsibilities ensure accountability throughout the project lifecycle.

Communication and quality assurance are foundational to our approach. Teams maintain a consistent rhythm with daily standups, weekly PM-PdM syncs, twice-weekly delivery team standups, and monthly stakeholder updates. Risks are tracked in a centralized Risk Register with clear assessment criteria and escalated through defined paths: team-level triage, PM escalation to Product Leads, and sponsor-level escalation for business-critical issues. Communication Coordinators ensure smooth information flow and maintain documentation, while Onboarding Specialists help new team members integrate quickly. Quality is ensured through unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, security scanning in CI, and manual QA for feature acceptance. All work follows our Definition of Done and includes automated testing before code review and merge.
