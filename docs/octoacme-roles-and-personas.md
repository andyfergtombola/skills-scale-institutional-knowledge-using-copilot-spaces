# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Risk Manager

### Role Summary
Risk Managers oversee risk identification, assessment, monitoring, and escalation procedures. They work closely with Project Managers and Stakeholders to ensure proactive risk mitigation and maintain the Risk Register.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document project risks during planning and execution
- Assess risk impact and likelihood in collaboration with the team
- Develop and track mitigation strategies and contingency plans
- Monitor risk status and trigger escalations when thresholds are exceeded
- Facilitate risk review sessions during weekly syncs and milestone reviews
- Maintain the Risk Register and ensure transparency across stakeholders

### Goals
- Minimize impact of risks on project delivery
- Enable data-driven decision-making around risk trade-offs
- Foster a culture of proactive risk identification and transparency

### Typical Communication
- Weekly risk reviews with Project Manager
- Risk status updates in stakeholder reports
- Escalation notifications to sponsors when needed
- Risk mitigation plans and decision logs

### Relationships with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Collaborates on risk tracking, escalation paths, and mitigation coordination
- **Developers**: Works with to identify technical risks and validate feasibility of mitigation plans
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on risk prioritization and trade-offs affecting scope or timeline
- **Stakeholders**: Provides risk visibility and escalates critical issues requiring executive decisions

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and uphold quality standards, review deliverables, and coordinate testing activities with development teams and stakeholders. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and maintain quality gates.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test strategies, and acceptance criteria
- Review deliverables for completeness, correctness, and usability
- Coordinate testing activities across unit, integration, and end-to-end levels
- Validate features against acceptance criteria before release
- Maintain test documentation and quality metrics
- Identify quality risks and collaborate on mitigation plans

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet or exceed quality standards
- Reduce defects and production incidents through comprehensive testing
- Enable fast, confident releases with robust quality gates

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with Developers on testing needs
- Weekly quality reports and metrics reviews
- Test plans and defect triage sessions
- Sign-off approvals for release readiness

### Relationships with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test strategy, reviews code for testability, and validates fixes
- **Project Manager**: Reports on quality metrics, testing progress, and blockers
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on acceptance criteria, prioritizes quality improvements
- **Risk Manager**: Identifies quality-related risks and validates mitigation effectiveness

---

## Communication Coordinator

### Role Summary
Communication Coordinators manage meeting schedules, documentation, and official communications between teams and stakeholders. They ensure information flows smoothly and stakeholders stay informed.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and facilitate team meetings (standups, planning, retrospectives)
- Maintain meeting notes, action items, and decision logs
- Coordinate stakeholder communications and status updates
- Manage project documentation and ensure single source of truth
- Distribute release notes, announcements, and important updates
- Track communication effectiveness and identify gaps

### Goals
- Ensure timely, clear, and consistent communication across all stakeholders
- Reduce information silos and communication overhead
- Maintain transparency and alignment throughout the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Meeting invitations and agenda distribution
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder briefings
- Documentation updates and announcements
- Coordination across all project personas

### Relationships with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Supports with meeting facilitation and status reporting
- **All Personas**: Acts as communication hub, ensuring information reaches the right audiences
- **Stakeholders**: Primary contact for coordinated updates and official communications
- **Onboarding Specialist**: Collaborates to ensure new team members have access to documentation

---

## Onboarding Specialist

### Role Summary
Onboarding Specialists guide new team members through processes, documentation, and tools. They work with HR and Project Managers to ensure smooth integration into project teams.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain onboarding documentation and checklists
- Guide new team members through project processes and workflows
- Coordinate with HR on administrative onboarding tasks
- Introduce new members to team roles, communication channels, and tools
- Collect feedback to improve the onboarding experience
- Track onboarding progress and ensure completion of required training

### Goals
- Reduce time-to-productivity for new team members
- Ensure consistent onboarding experience across all team members
- Identify and address gaps in documentation or training materials

### Typical Communication
- One-on-one onboarding sessions with new team members
- Coordination with HR and Project Manager on schedules
- Onboarding feedback surveys and improvement proposals
- Updates to onboarding documentation based on feedback

### Relationships with Other Roles
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates onboarding schedules and team integration
- **Communication Coordinator**: Ensures new members have access to documentation and communication channels
- **All Personas**: Introduces new team members and facilitates knowledge transfer
- **HR**: Partners on administrative tasks and overall employee onboarding
